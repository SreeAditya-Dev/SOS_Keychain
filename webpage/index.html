Questions

Frequently Asked

The SafeHer alarm produces 120dB of sound — that's louder than a chainsaw and can be heard from over 300 meters away. It's designed to immediately draw attention and deter any threat.

SafeHer connects to your smartphone via Bluetooth LE. When activated, the companion app uses your phone's GPS to send your exact coordinates to up to 5 emergency contacts via SMS and push notifications.

SafeHer is rated IP54, which means it's protected against dust and water splashes. It can handle rain and accidental spills, but should not be submerged in water.

Simply open the back panel, pop out the old CR2032 coin cell, and insert a new one. No tools needed. CR2032 batteries are available at any electronics or convenience store.

The 120dB alarm works independently without a phone connection. However, GPS sharing and SOS messaging require a Bluetooth connection to your smartphone with the SafeHer app installed.
